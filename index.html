How the sheets are assigned
  • Front, part 1: everyone draws the same smiley from 8 bytes.
  • Front, part 2: one of the selected 8 x 8 pictures, rotating down the list.
  • Front, part 3: students are grouped four at a time in list order. Each group shares one of the selected 16 x 16 pictures (cycling through them) and holds sections A, B, C, D of it.
  • Back, challenges 1 and 2: bytes written in decimal. Each student gets two different pictures from the selected pool, neither the same as that student's part 2 picture.
  • Back, challenge 3: picture ID and section are written in binary (0001 = A, 0010 = B, 0011 = C, 0100 = D), with bytes in binary or decimal. Groups differ from part 3, so partners change. Its pictures are never the same as part 2 or part 3 pictures.
