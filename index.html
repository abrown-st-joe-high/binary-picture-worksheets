Binary picture worksheets

Paste a class list. Download one PDF with an individualized two-sided worksheet per student, followed by the teacher key and reference pictures.

How the sheets are assigned
  • Front, part 1: everyone draws the same smiley from 8 bytes.
  • Front, part 2: one of 8 pictures (dog, cat, fish, bird, house, heart, tree, star), rotating down the list.
  • Front, part 3: students are grouped four at a time in list order. Each group shares a picture number (1 to 4) and holds sections A, B, C, D of a 16 x 16 picture.
  • Back, challenges 1 and 2: bytes written in decimal; each picture comes from a pool of 32.
  • Back, challenge 3: picture ID and section are written in binary (16 pictures; 0001 = A, 0010 = B, 0011 = C, 0100 = D). Groups here are different from part 3, so partners change.
